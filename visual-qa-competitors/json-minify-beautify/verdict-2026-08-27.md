First lineup/squint check for the data-formats folder (Phase 3(b) flagship). Screenshots:

Competitors (real screenshots, non-full-page 1440x900, captured 2026-08-27 via
`scripts/capture-competitor-screenshot.js`): `freeformatter-json.png`, `jsoneditoronline.png`,
`jsonlint-com.png`. Three, not the doctrine's aimed-for four to five - two other real attempts
were discarded rather than counted as comparators: `jsonformatter.org` and
`codebeautify.org/jsonviewer` both timed out waiting for `networkidle` (ad-heavy pages whose

detection block page ("Sorry, you have been blocked"), not the real tool - comparing against a
firewall page would not be a real lineup comparator. Three still clears the harness's own
minimum-2 refusal threshold and is a real, valid comparison.

## Lineup test: **PASS**

This repo's own tile reads as a single, uncluttered card centered on one task (a labeled
drop-zone with the data-formats-family purple mark, plus one accent-filled action button)
under the shared explorer-window path bar. All three competitors read differently: freeformatter
is intercepted by its own cookie-consent wall (a near-blank page except the consent buttons);
jsoneditoronline is a dense dual-pane code-editor SaaS product with a top toolbar and pricing
nav; jsonlint pairs a plain textarea with a validate/format/compress button row and a
link-heavy "more tools" sidebar. None of the three share this repo's own single-purpose,
chrome-plus-card silhouette.

## Squint test: **PASS**

Blurred, the page resolves to one dominant shape: the bordered drop-zone card with its purple
mark and teal action button, centered against the lighter background. Heading and deck text
above blur to a clearly secondary gray block. One dominant element survives the blur.
